ATX Total Properties: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

What is an SOP? A step-by-step "how we do it" guide. Follow these the same way every time. That's how a one-man shop looks and feels professional.

How to use this doc: Read the whole thing once. Then keep it open on your phone. When you're unsure, check the SOP.

SOP 1: Answering a New Lead

Goal: Answer every lead within 1 hour (faster wins the job).

  1. Answer calls with: "ATX Total Properties, this is Brandon."
  2. If you can't answer (on a job), text back within 1 hour: "Hey, this is Brandon with ATX Total Properties. I'm on a job right now. What's on your list? Feel free to send photos and I'll get you a price today."
  3. Get these 6 things (write them in your job app or the Lead Tracker):
    • Name
    • Phone and email
    • Address (check it's in your service area)
    • The list (ask for photos of each item)
    • When they'd like it done
    • How they heard about us (so you know what marketing works)
  4. Tell them what happens next: "I'll look this over and text you a price by [time]."

Phone script for the list: "Walk me through your list. For each thing, can you send me a photo? Do you already have the parts, like the TV mount or the light fixture, or do you want me to grab them?"

SOP 2: Giving a Price

  1. Estimate the time for each item. Add 20% for surprises and setup.
  2. Pick the package that fits: Quick Fix (2 hr, $190), Half-Day (4 hr, $360), Full-Day (8 hr, $680). Use flat rates where listed in the Business Plan.
  3. Add materials (at cost + 15%) if you're buying them.
  4. Send the price in writing (text or quote from your job app) the same day. Use this template:
"Hi [Name]! Here's your honey-do quote:
1. [Item]
2. [Item]
3. [Item]
Package: Half-Day Honey-Do, $360 (up to 4 hours). Materials: [amount or 'you provide'].
I have [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] open. Which works best?"
  1. Big projects (deck, tile, backsplash, anything over $1,000): go see it in person first. Written quote with a 50% deposit to book.
  2. If they don't reply in 2 days, follow up once (SOP 8).

SOP 3: Scheduling and Confirmations

  1. Put every job in the calendar with: address, list, price, materials needed, customer phone.
  2. Block 30 minutes of drive time between jobs.
  3. Day before (by 6 PM): Send confirmation text: "Hi [Name], Brandon here. Confirming tomorrow at [time]. I'll text when I'm on the way. Please make sure the work areas are clear and pets are put away. See you then!"
  4. On the way: Text "On my way, see you in about [X] minutes!"
  5. Running late? Text before you're late, not after.

SOP 4: Before You Leave the House

  1. Read the job list again.
  2. Check the tool list for each item. Load the truck.
  3. Pick up materials (keep receipts, take a photo of each).
  4. Pack: drop cloths, shoe covers, shop vac, trash bags, rags, first aid kit.
  5. Wear the branded shirt. Clean clothes. You are the brand.

SOP 5: On the Job

  1. Arrive 5 minutes early. Park so you don't block their driveway.
  2. Introduce: Smile, handshake, "Hi, I'm Brandon. Ready to knock out your list?" Put on shoe covers.
  3. Walkthrough (5 min): Go over every item together. Confirm what "done" looks like. Confirm the price. Ask if anything was added. Any new items get priced before you start.
  4. Before photos of each item (SOP 13).
  5. Protect the area: drop cloths down, move furniture carefully.
  6. Do the work. Biggest or hardest item first.
  7. Something unexpected (rot, bad wiring, a leak)? Stop. Show the customer, explain options and price. Never surprise them on the bill.
  8. Clean up each area as you finish it. Vacuum. Take your trash.
  9. After photos.
  10. Final walkthrough: Show the customer each finished item. Ask: "Is everything how you wanted it?"

SOP 6: Getting Paid

  1. Send the invoice before you leave (job app, Square, Venmo Business, Zelle, or card).
  2. Say: "I just sent your invoice. You can pay by card or Zelle, whatever's easiest."
  3. Unpaid after 3 days? Friendly reminder text. After 7 days, call.
  4. Deposits (big projects): 50% to book, rest at finish.

SOP 7: Asking for Reviews and Referrals

Every happy customer. No exceptions. Reviews are the #1 way new customers find you.

  1. During the final walkthrough, when they're smiling: "I'm a small local business, and reviews help me a ton. Would you mind leaving me a quick Google review? I'll text you the link."
  2. Send the review link within 1 hour: "Thanks again, [Name]! Here's the link if you have a minute: [Google review link]. If you know anyone with a honey-do list, I give $25 off your next visit for every referral who books."
  3. Reply to every review within 24 hours, by name.

SOP 9: When Something Goes Wrong

  1. Stay calm and listen. Don't argue. Say: "I hear you. Let's make it right."
  2. Look at the problem in person within 48 hours.
  3. If it was our work: fix it free. No excuses.
  4. If you broke something: tell the customer right away, take photos, and call your insurance if it's big.
  5. Bad online review: reply politely once, offer to fix it, take the conversation to the phone.
  6. Write down what happened and what you'll do differently.

SOP 10: Safety and "We Don't Do That" List

Safety basics: Safety glasses with power tools. Ladder on flat ground, 3 points of contact. Turn off the breaker before touching any fixture and test with a voltage tester. Turn off the water before plumbing. Never work on a roof alone.

We don't do (refer out):

  • Anything requiring a plumbing or electrical license (confirm limits with TSBPE and TDLR)
  • Gas lines, HVAC, roofing, structural/load-bearing walls, asbestos or mold removal
  • Wainscoting, attic insulation, garage floor coating, baby proofing, window glass repair

Script: "That one needs a licensed [plumber/electrician]. I work with a great one, want their number?"

SOP 11: Money and Bookkeeping (every Friday, 30 minutes)

  1. Check every job this week is invoiced and paid.
  2. Upload receipt photos to your bookkeeping app or a "Receipts 2026" Google Drive folder.
  3. Move 25% of this week's profit to the tax savings account.
  4. Write down the 3 numbers: money in, jobs booked next week, new reviews.
  5. Pay yourself from the business account (a set amount, same day each week).

Never pay personal bills from the business account.

SOP 12: Tools, Truck, and Supplies (every Sunday, 20 minutes)

  1. Clean out the truck. Trash out.
  2. Restock the consumables bin: screws, anchors, caulk, tape, sandpaper, blades, batteries, drop cloths, shoe covers, trash bags.
  3. Charge all batteries.
  4. Check next week's jobs and buy what you need.
  5. Only buy new tools when a paid job needs them.

SOP 13: Photos and Content on Every Job

Every job is free marketing. Takes 3 minutes total.

  1. Ask: "Mind if I take before and after photos for my page? I never show your address."
  2. Before photo: same angle, good light.
  3. After photo: exact same angle.
  4. One 10 to 20 second video (drill going in, TV going up, the reveal).
  5. Never show house numbers, faces (unless they say yes), or personal items.
  6. Drop photos into the "Content" folder in Google Drive the same night.
