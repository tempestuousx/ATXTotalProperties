ATX Total Properties: Content and Marketing Plan

Covers: November 2, 2026 to January 22, 2027 (12 weeks). After that, repeat the weekly rhythm and use the Idea Bank.

1. The Simple Strategy

People hire handymen they trust. Trust comes from 3 things, so every post does one of these:

  1. Show the work (before and after, quick videos)
  2. Be helpful (tips that make Brandon look like the expert)
  3. Be a real person (Brandon's face, his story, the truck, the dog, the coffee)

Brand voice: friendly neighbor who's really good at fixing stuff. Short sentences. A little humor. Never salesy. Always end with an easy next step ("Text me your list").
