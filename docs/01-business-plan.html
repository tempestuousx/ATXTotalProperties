1. The Big Idea (one paragraph)

Everyone has a list. The loose cabinet door, the TV still sitting on the floor, the dripping faucet, the pictures that never got hung. Big contractors don't want these small jobs, and most people don't have the time or tools. ATX Total Properties is the honey-do list specialist. Customers send their whole list, Brandon gives one clear price, shows up on time, and knocks out the entire list in one visit. Friendly, clean, and done right.

Important: Texas license check (do this before offering these)

In Texas, some plumbing and electrical work legally requires a licensed plumber or electrician, and the City of Austin requires permits for some of it. Before advertising these services, Brandon should call or check the websites of the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE) and Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) and ask exactly what a handyman may do:

  • Sink, faucet, toilet install and repair, minor plumbing leaks
  • Light fixture install, attic fan install, garage door opener install (hard-wired)

Until confirmed, the safe move: do the non-licensed parts (assembly, mounting, caulking, swapping a shower head) and refer licensed work to a trusted plumber and electrician. Build those referral partnerships early (they can send work back to you too). This protects Brandon, his insurance, and his customers.

8. Marketing Plan (summary)

Full details are in the Content and Marketing Plan doc. The short version, in order of importance:

  1. Google Business Profile with photos and reviews. Goal: 25 five-star reviews in 90 days.
  2. Ask every happy customer for a review and a referral. Give $25 off their next visit for each referral that books.
  3. Nextdoor and Facebook neighborhood groups. Helpful posts, not spam.
  4. Social media: Facebook and Instagram, 3 to 4 posts per week. Before and after photos win.
  5. Realtor and property manager relationships. Visit 2 offices per week.
  6. Yard signs at every job (with permission) and a magnet on the truck.
