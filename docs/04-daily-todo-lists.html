ATX Total Properties: Daily To-Do Lists

How to use: Every morning, open this doc and do today's list. Check off each box (in Google Docs: Format > Bullets & numbering > Checklist, or just type an X). If you miss a day, don't skip it. Do it tomorrow and keep going.

Part 1 is the 30-Day Launch Plan (starts Monday, October 26, 2026). Part 2 is the Daily Routine you'll use forever after.

Part 1: 30-Day Launch Plan

Week 1: Get Legal and Set Up (Oct 26 to Nov 1)

Day 1 (Mon Oct 26)

  • ☐ Read the Business Plan start to finish
  • ☐ Decide your service area (cities you'll drive to)
  • ☐ File the Texas LLC online at the Secretary of State (SOSDirect), Form 205, $300
  • ☐ Make a free Gmail just for the business (example: atxtotalproperties@gmail.com)

Day 2 (Tue Oct 27)

  • ☐ Get a business phone number (Google Voice is free)
  • ☐ Set your voicemail: "You've reached ATX Total Properties, this is Brandon. Text me your list and photos and I'll get back to you today."
  • ☐ Get 3 general liability insurance quotes (search "handyman insurance Texas")

Day 3 (Wed Oct 28)

  • ☐ Call TSBPE (plumbing) and TDLR (electrical). Ask exactly what a handyman can do without a license. Write down the answers.
  • ☐ Update your services list based on what they said
  • ☐ Text 2 local licensed plumbers and 2 electricians about swapping referrals

Day 4 (Thu Oct 29)

  • ☐ Read the SOPs doc start to finish
  • ☐ Save the text templates (quote, confirmation, review request) in your phone's notes or as text shortcuts
  • ☐ Pick a job app: try Jobber or Housecall Pro free trial, or start with Google Calendar + Square

Day 5 (Fri Oct 30)

  • ☐ Order business cards, 10 yard signs, and a truck magnet (files are in the brand pack)
  • ☐ Order 3 branded shirts (badge on the front or chest)
  • ☐ Pick your insurance and buy it

Day 6 (Sat Oct 31)

  • ☐ Create your Google Business Profile (business.google.com). Add logo, hours, services, service area.
  • ☐ Upload 5 photos (you, your truck, tools, any past work)

Day 7 (Sun Nov 1)

  • ☐ Create Facebook page and Instagram account. Upload profile picture and cover from the brand pack.
  • ☐ Schedule Week 1 posts in Meta Business Suite (captions are in the Content Plan)
  • ☐ Restock and organize the truck (SOP 12)

Part 2: Daily Routine (use every day after the launch)

☀️ Morning (20 minutes, before the first job)

  • ☐ Check calls, texts, emails, Facebook and Instagram messages. Reply to every lead.
  • ☐ Look at today's jobs: addresses, lists, materials
  • ☐ Send "On my way" text before each job
  • ☐ Check today's post went live

🔨 Every Job

  • ☐ Arrive 5 minutes early, branded shirt on
  • ☐ Walkthrough and confirm price
  • ☐ Before photos
  • ☐ Do the work, clean as you go
  • ☐ After photos and a short video
  • ☐ Final walkthrough with the customer
  • ☐ Invoice sent and paid before you leave
  • ☐ Ask for the review, send the link

🌙 Evening (20 minutes)

  • ☐ Send quotes for any new leads (same day!)
  • ☐ Send tomorrow's confirmation texts (by 6 PM)
  • ☐ Drop today's photos into the Content folder
  • ☐ Snap receipt photos
  • ☐ Answer one "need a handyman" post online (5 min)

  • ☐ Count reviews, jobs, money in, and where leads came from
  • ☐ Pick one thing to improve this month
  • ☐ Text past customers from 3 months ago (seasonal check-in)
  • ☐ Check prices: booked more than 2 weeks out? Raise rates $10/hr.
  • ☐ Pay quarterly estimated taxes (January, April, June, September; ask your CPA for exact dates)
