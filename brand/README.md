# ATX Total Properties Brand Pack

**Tagline:** Your honey-do list, done.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Shop Black | `#161616` | Backgrounds, main text |
| Austin Ember | `#E2692B` | Main brand color, buttons, "ATX" |
| Honey | `#F2B33D` | Accents, highlights, underlines |
| Sawdust | `#F6EFE4` | Light backgrounds, text on dark |
| Flannel Gray | `#5E656C` | Secondary text |

## Fonts (free, Google Fonts)
- **Archivo Black**: headlines
- **Oswald Bold**: labels, taglines (ALL CAPS, wide spacing)
- **Inter**: body text

## Folders
- `logos/svg` and `logos/png`: badge, lockups, wordmark, icon marks, one-color versions
- `logos/favicon`: website and app icons
- `icons/`: 37 service icons in color, white (for dark backgrounds) and black
- `kit/`: profile picture, Facebook cover, Instagram post and story templates, business card, yard sign
- `source/`: original illustration and the cleaned-up version (team logo removed from the cap)
- `scripts/`: rebuild everything (`python3 scripts/build_logos.py` etc. from this folder)

## Quick rules
1. Use the **badge** for profile pictures, shirts, truck, yard signs.
2. Use the **icon mark** (house + check) when the badge is too small to read (under ~150px).
3. Keep clear space around logos equal to the height of the "A" in ATX.
4. Do not stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo.
5. Replace `(512) 000-0000` and the email on templates with the real info.
